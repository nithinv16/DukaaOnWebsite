## 2. Google Search Console (GSC) Checklist
This is the most critical step to get indexed.
1. **Login:** Go to [search.google.com/search-console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. **Add Property:** If you haven't, add `https://dukaaon.in`. Verify it via DNS (TXT record) or by uploading an HTML file.
3. **Submit Sitemap:**
   - Go to "Sitemaps" in the left sidebar.
   - Enter `sitemap.xml` and click Submit.
   - *Result:* Google will see all your new pages (Locations, Blogs, etc.) and queue them for crawling.
4. **URL Inspection:**
   - Paste a specific URL like `https://dukaaon.in/locations/kerala` into the top search bar.
   - Click **"Request Indexing"**. This forces Google to look at it priority.

## 3. Increasing Visibility (Beyond SEO)
Since you are focusing on **Kerala** and **Hyperlocal**, you need "off-page" signals.

### A. Google My Business (GMB) - Crucial
- Create a GMB profile for "DukaaOn" in your key operational hubs (e.g., Kochi office, or virtual offices in districts).
- **Service Areas:** Add Ranni, Pathanamthitta, Idukki as specific "Service Areas" in your GMB settings.
- **Posts:** Post updates on GMB using the same content: "Stock on Credit available in Ranni".

### B. WhatsApp Seeding
- Take the link `dukaaon.in/blog/stock-on-credit-for-kerala-retailers`.
- Share it in Kerala Vyapari Vyavasayi Ekopana Samithi (KVVES) WhatsApp groups.
- *Caption:* "Struggling with cash flow? Check how DukaaOn gives stock on credit for Kirana stores in Pathanamthitta."

### C. Local Backlinks
- Partner with local Kerala business news portals or blogs.
- Ask them to feature your story: "Startup enabling credit for Idukki high-range merchants."

## 4. Monitoring
- **Check GSC Weekly:** Look at the "Performance" tab. See what queries people are typing (e.g., "wholesale nearby").
- **Iterate:** If you see people searching for "Rice wholesale", create a specifically optimized page or blog post for it.
